## Official product entitlements

Official Community builds may enforce a tier of up to 20 hosted root domains.
An official Unlimited entitlement removes that limit for one installation.
The copyright licence does not itself supply a paid entitlement or commercial
support, and this change does not alter activation or domain-limit behavior.

Entitlements are signed and verified locally using a bundled public key. An
expiry or licensing outage must not delete, suspend or alter existing customer
websites, DNS or mail. Private licence and update signing keys are operational
secrets and are never included in public releases.
