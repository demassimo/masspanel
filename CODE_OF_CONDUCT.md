# Code of conduct

MassPanel contributors and community members must be respectful, constructive and professional. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing and publication of another person's credentials or private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers should protect a reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible and handle reports fairly.
